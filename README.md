# Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle 🌸

> A safe sisterhood space where girls can connect, share, laugh, learn, and grow together.

**Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle** is a girl-led online community created to give girls a space where they can be themselves, have honest conversations, build meaningful friendships, discover opportunities, and grow with other girls.

The community is built around one simple belief:

**Girls should not have to figure everything out alone.**

---

## 🌸 About Girls' Unplugged

Girls' Unplugged is a growing sisterhood for girls across Africa.

The community creates room for:

- Honest conversations
- Friendship and connection
- Confidence building
- Emotional wellbeing
- Leadership development
- Learning and personal growth
- Technology and creativity
- Fun and community activities
- Girl-led conversations
- Access to useful opportunities and resources

The community currently brings together **90+ girls across 6+ African countries** and continues to grow.

---

## ✨ What We Do

Girls' Unplugged creates different experiences throughout the year, including:

### Motivation Monday

A space to start the week with encouragement, reflection, and motivation.

### Wellness Wednesday

Conversations and reminders around wellbeing, self-care, emotional health, and checking in with yourself.

### Fun Friday

Games, laughter, activities, and moments where girls can simply enjoy being girls.

### Reflection Saturday

A slower space for reflection, gratitude, lessons, and personal growth.

### Guest Conversations

Interactive conversations with invited speakers and women who can share real experiences, lessons, and practical advice.

Guest conversations currently take place on the **first Friday of each month from 7:00–8:30 PM WAT** through Google Meet.

### Quarterly Calls

Virtual community calls where Bloomies can actually see and connect with one another.

### Special Initiatives

Girl-led activities, campaigns, challenges, projects, and other experiences created around the needs and interests of the community.
