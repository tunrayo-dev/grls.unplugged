About Girls' Unplugged

A sisterhood created by girls, for girls.

Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle is a growing community where girls can show up as themselves, have honest conversations, build meaningful friendships, and grow together.

Our Story

It started with a simple idea: girls shouldn't have to figure everything out alone.

Girls' Unplugged began with a desire to create the kind of space many girls need but do not always have — a space where you can talk honestly, laugh without pretending, ask questions without feeling judged, and meet girls who understand that growing up can be a lot.

What began as a simple online sisterhood has grown into a community of girls from different parts of Africa, connected by the same desire to feel seen, supported, heard, and encouraged.

We believe community does not have to start with a huge budget, a fancy office, or a perfect plan. Sometimes it starts with a girl deciding to create the space she wishes existed.

Why We Exist

Because being a girl can be a lot.

There are questions we are scared to ask, feelings we do not always know how to explain, dreams we sometimes shrink, and moments when we simply need someone to say, "I get it."

01

A place to be real

You do not have to have everything figured out before you walk through our doors. We make room for honest conversations, questions, emotions, mistakes, and everyday girlhood.

02

A place to connect

We bring girls together across backgrounds and locations so friendships can grow beyond screens, school walls, neighbourhoods, and familiar circles.

03

A place to grow

From confidence and emotional wellness to leadership, learning, and self-expression, we create moments that help girls discover more of who they can become.

Our Purpose

We are building more than a group chat.

Girls' Unplugged is designed to be a space where connection becomes a starting point for growth.

Through conversations, community activities, guest sessions, games, reflections, and shared experiences, we create opportunities for girls to learn from one another and discover that their voices matter.

We are interested in the small things that make a difference too: checking in on a girl, celebrating a win, listening when someone needs to talk, introducing two girls who might become friends, or simply reminding someone that she is not alone.

What We Believe

The values behind the Circle.

01

Belonging

Every girl deserves spaces where she feels welcomed, respected, and included.

02

Honesty

We encourage conversations that are genuine, thoughtful, and grounded in real experiences.

03

Growth

We are all works in progress. There is room here to learn, unlearn, try again, and become.

04

Sisterhood

We believe girls can support, celebrate, challenge, and champion one another.

05

Joy

Growth does not have to be serious all the time. Laughter, games, friendship, and fun matter too.

06

Courage

We encourage girls to use their voices, pursue their dreams, ask questions, and take up space.

How We Show Up

Community can look like many things.

We keep the Circle active through a mix of conversations, reflection, learning, friendship, and fun.

Mondays

Motivation Monday

A little encouragement to start the week with intention, confidence, and a reminder that your goals still matter.

Wednesdays

Wellness Wednesday

Conversations and reminders around emotional wellness, self-care, healthy habits, and taking care of yourself.

Fridays

Fun Friday

Games, laughter, playful conversations, and those random moments that make a community feel like home.

Saturdays

Reflection Saturday

A chance to pause, look back on the week, appreciate progress, and think about what comes next.

A Note From Our Founder

“I wanted to create the kind of space girls could simply be themselves. ”

Girls' Unplugged was created from a very simple belief: girls deserve to have people around them who remind them that their experiences matter.

I wanted a community that could hold the serious conversations and the silly ones. A place where we could talk about dreams, friendships, confidence, emotions, school, growing up, and all the little things in between.

This Circle is not about being perfect. It is about showing up, learning together, supporting one another, and creating room for every girl to bloom in her own way.

With love,
Founder, Girls' Unplugged
