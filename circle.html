THE BLOOM & GLOW CIRCLE

This is more than a group chat. This is sisterhood.

Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle is an online sisterhood created for girls who want a place to connect, be themselves, have honest conversations, make friends, learn, laugh and grow together.

Join the Sisterhood See What's Inside

WELCOME TO THE CIRCLE

A place where you don't have to figure everything out alone.

Growing up can be exciting, confusing, beautiful, awkward and overwhelming — sometimes all at once.

There are things girls think about but don't always know who to talk to about. Questions about friendships, confidence, school, emotions, identity, leadership, growing up and everything in between.

Girls' Unplugged exists because we believe girls deserve a space where they can have those conversations without feeling like they have to pretend to have everything together.

Here, you can show up as you are, meet girls from different backgrounds and places, and grow alongside people who understand that becoming yourself is a journey.

OUR COMMUNITY CULTURE

We want this to feel safe, warm and real.

A sisterhood only works when everyone helps create the kind of space they want to belong to.

RESPECT

We respect each other.

Different experiences, opinions, backgrounds and personalities can exist in the same room. Respect is non-negotiable.

KINDNESS

We lead with kindness.

You never know what someone else is carrying. We choose empathy, encouragement and care over unnecessary judgment.

SAFETY

We protect the space.

Personal stories shared within the community should be treated with care. We do not use vulnerability as entertainment.

PARTICIPATION

You can participate your way.

Some days you may have a lot to say. Some days you may simply read and listen. Both are okay.

HONESTY

We make room for real life.

You do not have to perform perfection here. We can celebrate the wins while still acknowledging that life gets hard.

SISTERHOOD

We root for each other.

Your growth does not take away from mine. There is room for all of us to bloom.

WHY THE CIRCLE MATTERS

We are not trying to create another perfect-looking girls' community.

Girls' Unplugged was created from a simple idea: girls should have somewhere they can come as they are.

That means the community can be thoughtful without being overly formal, educational without feeling like school and fun without losing its purpose.

The Circle is intentionally girl-centred. We listen to what girls want to talk about, what they are experiencing and what kinds of connections they want to build.

It is not about collecting titles or trying to make everyone look the same.

It is about creating a space where girls can bloom without having to bloom alone.

OUR COMMUNITY STORY

It started small. The feeling was always big.

Girls' Unplugged started with an idea and what was available at the time. No huge office. No complicated setup. Just a desire to create a space where girls could connect.

What began as a simple online sisterhood has grown into a community of girls connecting across different African countries.

Along the way, we have had conversations, played games, celebrated wins, checked in on each other, welcomed new girls and created memories that could never have happened if someone had not decided to start.

And we are still growing.

A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

I wanted to create the kind of space I knew girls could use.

Girls' Unplugged was born from the belief that connection can change how a girl experiences growing up.

Sometimes one conversation can make you feel less alone. One person believing in you can change how you see yourself. One community can remind you that there are other girls figuring things out too.

That is what I hope the Bloom & Glow Circle continues to be — a place where girls can connect honestly, discover themselves, support one another and keep becoming.

With love,
Motunrayo Linda Idowu
