Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Girls Unplugged, the Bloom & Glow Circle, and how you can be part of the sisterhood.

What is Girls Unplugged?

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle is an online sisterhood created for girls to connect, share, laugh, learn, and grow together.

Who can join?

Girls Unplugged is created for girls who want a supportive space to connect with other girls and grow together.

Is Girls Unplugged free?

Yes. Joining the Bloom & Glow Circle is free.

Where is the community based?

Girls Unplugged is an online community connecting girls from different parts of Africa.

What do members do in the circle?

We have conversations, games, wellness activities, reflection moments, guest sessions, community calls, and other activities designed around the interests and needs of our members.

What are the weekly activities?

Our weekly vibes include Motivation Monday, Wellness Wednesday, Fun Friday, and Reflection Saturday.

Do you have guest speakers?

Yes. We invite guests to share their experiences, knowledge, stories, opportunities, and advice with the community.

How can I join the sisterhood?

Head over to our Get Involved page and follow the steps to join the community.

Can I collaborate with Girls Unplugged?

Absolutely. If you have an idea, opportunity, skill, project, or collaboration that could support the community, we'd love to hear from you.

How can I contact the team?

You can reach us through our Contact page or send us an email.
