THE BASICS

About Girls' Unplugged

The quick answers to help you understand what we're building.

What is Girls' Unplugged? +

Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle is an online sisterhood created for girls to connect, share, laugh, learn, and grow together.

It's a space for honest conversations, friendships, confidence, emotional wellness, leadership, fun, and everything in between.

Why was Girls' Unplugged created? +

Because girls deserve spaces where they don't have to pretend they have everything figured out.

Girls' Unplugged was created to make room for connection, honest conversations, support, learning, friendship, and joy. We want girls to know that they are not alone in the things they are navigating.

When was Girls' Unplugged founded? +

Girls' Unplugged was founded on September 29, 2025.

What started as a simple idea for a girls' community has continued to grow into a sisterhood of girls connecting across African countries.

Who is Girls' Unplugged for? +

Girls' Unplugged is created for girls who want a safe and welcoming space to connect with other girls, have meaningful conversations, learn, grow, and simply be themselves.

Whether you're outgoing, quiet, figuring things out, looking for friends, or just curious about the community, there is room for you here.

Where are the girls in the community from? +

Our Bloomies are girls from different African countries and backgrounds.

The community has connected girls from countries including Nigeria, Ghana, Sierra Leone, The Gambia, Uganda, Kenya, South Africa, and others.

JOINING THE CIRCLE

Thinking about joining us?

Here's what you should know before you step into the Circle.

How do I join Girls' Unplugged? +

Head over to our Get Involved page and choose the option that fits you.

You can also reach out to us directly through our Contact page if you have a question before joining.

Do I have to be from Nigeria to join? +

Nope! 🌍

Girls' Unplugged is a growing African sisterhood. Girls from different African countries and backgrounds are welcome to connect with the community.

Do I have to be very active to belong? +

Not at all.

You don't have to be the girl who is always typing, speaking, or joining every activity. You can participate at a pace that feels comfortable for you.

We simply encourage everyone to respect the space and the girls in it.

Do I have to pay to join? +

Girls' Unplugged is a community space, and joining the sisterhood itself does not require a membership payment.

If a specific future activity ever has different participation requirements, we will communicate them clearly.

What happens after I join? +

You become part of the Bloomies — our community of girls growing and connecting together.

You'll get to participate in community conversations, weekly activities, games, learning moments, guest conversations, and other activities shared with the Circle.

INSIDE THE CIRCLE

What actually happens here?

More than just “hi guys” in a group chat. 😂

What activities do you have? +

Our community activities include Motivation Monday, Wellness Wednesday, Fun Friday, Reflection Saturday, game nights, community conversations, guest speaker sessions, quarterly community calls, and special initiatives.

Activities can also evolve as the community grows and girls share what they want to see.

What is Motivation Monday? +

Motivation Monday is our little reminder that a new week doesn't have to mean having everything figured out.

It's a space for encouragement, intentions, reflections, and positive energy as Bloomies step into a new week.

What is Wellness Wednesday? +

Wellness Wednesday creates room to check in with yourself.

Conversations can touch on emotional wellness, self-care, healthy habits, confidence, relationships, and other parts of navigating life as a girl.

What happens on Fun Friday? +

We have fun! That's literally the point. 😭💜

Fun Friday can include games, challenges, random conversations, activities, and sometimes an anonymous night where girls can submit things they want to share without attaching their names.

What is Reflection Saturday? +

Reflection Saturday is a chance to slow down and think about the week — what happened, what we learnt, what we're grateful for, and what we want to carry into the next week.

How often do you have community calls? +

We have community calls quarterly.

They're a chance for Bloomies to actually see and interact with one another beyond the everyday group conversations.

GUEST CONVERSATIONS

Curious about our speakers?

We bring people into the Circle to have conversations that girls actually want to have.

When do guest speaker sessions happen? +

Guest speaker conversations are held on the first Friday of the month, from 7:00–8:30 PM WAT, usually through Google Meet.

Who chooses the topics? +

The girls help shape them.

We want conversations to reflect what Bloomies are actually curious about, experiencing, questioning, or trying to understand.

What kind of speakers do you invite? +

We look for people with experiences, skills, stories, or perspectives that can create meaningful conversations with girls.

Topics can range from confidence and identity to education, careers, leadership, technology, creativity, wellness, and everyday life.

Can I become a speaker? +

Yes! If you have an experience, skill, story, or perspective you think could be valuable to the Bloomies, we'd love to hear from you.

Visit Get Involved or Contact Us to express your interest.

SAFETY & RESPECT

Is this actually a safe space?

Creating a welcoming community means being intentional about how we treat one another.

What are the rules inside the community? +

We expect everyone to treat one another with respect, kindness, honesty, and care.

Bullying, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, harmful behaviour, and deliberate attempts to make another girl feel unsafe are not welcome here.

Can I share something personal in the community? +

You can share what you feel comfortable sharing.

At the same time, you should never feel pressured to reveal personal information. We encourage girls to protect their privacy and respect the privacy of others.

Can I report something that makes me uncomfortable? +

Yes.

If something happens in the community that makes you uncomfortable or unsafe, please reach out to the Girls' Unplugged team directly through our Contact page.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact an appropriate trusted adult or local emergency service as well.

Should I share my private information in the group? +

Please be careful with personal information online.

Avoid sharing passwords, financial information, private addresses, sensitive documents, or other information that could put you at risk.

You can learn more about how we approach privacy on our Privacy page .
