IT TAKES A COMMUNITY

Girls' Unplugged is not something one person builds alone.

A sisterhood becomes meaningful because people show up for one another.

Some girls join because they want connection. Some share their ideas. Some volunteer their time. Some bring a story, a skill, an opportunity, or a conversation that another girl needed.

There is no single way to contribute. Start where you are.
