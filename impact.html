OUR IMPACT

The impact is in the girls.

We are building something that cannot be measured only by numbers. It is in the girl who finds her people, the girl who finally speaks up, the friendship that starts in a group chat, and the moments that remind girls they are not alone.

See Our Impact Explore the Circle

WHY IMPACT MATTERS

Sometimes, impact looks like simply knowing you belong.

Girls' Unplugged was created around a simple idea: girls should not have to navigate every season of life feeling alone.

So our impact is not only about how many girls are in the community. It is also about the conversations they have, the confidence they build, the friendships they form and the opportunities they discover.

We are still growing, learning and documenting that journey. Every Bloomie who shows up becomes part of the story.

Small moments can mean a lot.

WHERE IT SHOWS UP

Our impact has many faces.

We create different kinds of moments because girls need more than one kind of support.

01

Connection

Girls meet people they may never have crossed paths with otherwise and build friendships across different places and experiences.

The Circle creates a place where “I thought I was the only one” can become “wait, you too?”

02

Confidence

Through conversations, reflections and opportunities to share, girls get more room to express themselves and use their voices.

Sometimes confidence begins with one small decision to speak.

03

Personal growth

From wellness conversations to leadership, careers and everyday life, the community gives girls space to keep learning.

We want growth to feel possible, practical and personal.

04

Joy

Impact does not always have to look serious. Sometimes girls simply need somewhere to laugh, play games and have fun.

Joy is part of community too.

05

Voice

Girls get opportunities to ask questions, share opinions, tell their stories and contribute to the direction of their community.

Their voices are part of the Circle.

06

Belonging

At the heart of everything is the hope that a girl can enter the Circle and feel a little less alone.

That feeling matters.

THE COMMUNITY STORY

It started with an idea. Then girls showed up.

Girls' Unplugged began as a simple idea: create a space where girls could connect with one another and talk about the things that actually matter to them.

From conversations and wellness check-ins to game nights and guest sessions, the community has continued to grow through the participation of the girls themselves.

What began small has become a growing sisterhood of girls across Africa.

And we are only getting started.

GIRLS ARE PART OF THE PROCESS

We don't just build for girls. We build with them.

The girls in the Circle are not passive participants. Their interests, questions and ideas help shape the community experience.

Topics for guest conversations are shaped by what girls want to discuss. Activities create room for participation. Conversations make space for different perspectives.

That is important to us because a community should not simply speak to girls. It should listen to them too.
