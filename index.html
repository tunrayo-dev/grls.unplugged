So... what's Girls' Unplugged?

A place to be real.

Sometimes you just need a space where you can talk without pretending you have everything figured out.

Connect

Meet girls from different backgrounds and places, build genuine friendships, and discover that you are not the only one navigating growing up, school, dreams, friendships, or everything in between.

Speak

From the things we usually overthink to the questions we sometimes feel shy to ask, the Circle makes room for honest conversations, different perspectives, laughter, and vulnerability.

Grow

Build confidence, learn new things, develop leadership skills, explore opportunities, and become more intentional about the girl and woman you are becoming.

Why we exist

Because growing up can feel like a lot.

There are days when you feel confident and days when you question everything. Days when you have big dreams and days when you don't even know what your next step should be.

There are conversations you want to have, questions you want to ask, things you want to learn, and feelings you want to express without worrying about being judged.

Girls' Unplugged was created to make room for those moments.

We believe girls deserve spaces where they can be heard, supported, celebrated, challenged to grow, and reminded that their voices matter.

Inside the Circle

More than a group chat.

Girls' Unplugged is built around intentional conversations, connection, fun, learning, reflection, and opportunities to simply be yourself.

Monday

Motivation Monday

We start the week with encouragement, reminders, reflections, and little nudges to keep moving.

Wednesday

Wellness Wednesday

We check in with ourselves, talk about wellbeing, and make space for conversations that help us take better care of ourselves.

Friday

Fun Friday

Games, laughter, challenges, anonymous conversations, random questions, and the moments that remind us not everything has to be serious.

Saturday

Reflection Saturday

We slow down, look back on the week, celebrate little wins, and think about what we want to carry into the next one.

A note from the founder

“I wanted girls to know they don't have to do growing up alone.”

Girls' Unplugged started with a simple idea: create the kind of space where girls could talk, connect, laugh, learn, and feel seen.

What began as an idea has grown into a sisterhood of girls from different places and backgrounds, each bringing her own story, personality, dreams, questions, and experiences.

The Circle is still growing, and so are we. But the heart remains the same — creating room for girls to become.
