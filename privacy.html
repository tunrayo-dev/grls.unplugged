YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy, explained simply.

We want you to understand what information Girls' Unplugged may receive, how we may use it, and the choices you have.

PRIVACY POLICY

A little clarity goes a long way.

This Privacy Policy explains how Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle may handle information when you visit this website, contact us, or participate in our community.

We believe privacy should not be hidden behind complicated language, so we've kept this as clear and practical as possible.

01

Information We May Receive

Depending on how you interact with Girls' Unplugged, we may receive information such as your name, email address, social media username, organisation, or the details you choose to include in a message.

We only ask for information that is relevant to the reason you are reaching out.

02

How We Use Information

Information you voluntarily share may be used to respond to your message, communicate with you about Girls' Unplugged activities, understand community needs, or coordinate an opportunity, collaboration, volunteering, speaking engagement, or partnership.

We do not intend to collect information simply because we can. Information should have a meaningful reason to be there.

03

The Girls' Unplugged Community

Girls' Unplugged is a community built around connection, conversation, learning, fun, and sisterhood.

Some community activities may take place on platforms operated by other companies, such as WhatsApp, Google Meet, Instagram, TikTok, or other services we may use from time to time.

Those platforms have their own privacy policies, terms, and settings. Information shared directly on those platforms may therefore be handled according to their respective practices.

04

Community Conversations & Safety

We want Girls' Unplugged to remain a space where girls can participate without feeling pressured to reveal personal information.

Please avoid sharing highly sensitive information publicly, including passwords, financial information, exact home addresses, or information that could put you or another person at risk.

If you participate in community conversations, remember that other participants may see what you choose to share.

05

Anonymous Night

Girls' Unplugged may use anonymous question tools for activities such as Anonymous Night.

If you submit something through an external anonymous-question service, that submission is also subject to the privacy practices of that service.

Anonymous does not always mean completely untraceable, so please use good judgment when deciding what to submit.

06

External Links

Our website may contain links to websites, platforms, registration forms, resources, social media pages, or other services that are operated by third parties.

Once you leave the Girls' Unplugged website, the privacy practices of the destination service apply. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

07

Cookies & Analytics

This website is currently designed as a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. We do not use this Privacy Policy to claim that analytics, cookies, or tracking tools are active when they have not been set up.

If analytics or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy may be updated to explain what information those services collect and why.

08

Contact Forms

The contact page is currently part of a static website. A working submission service or backend may be connected in the future.

If a third-party form service is connected, information submitted through that service may be processed according to both the service's privacy policy and the arrangements made by Girls' Unplugged.

We will aim to use reputable services and collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the purpose of the form.

09

Young People & Children

Girls' Unplugged is a community created with girls and young people in mind. Because some members may be under the age of 18, we take privacy and safety seriously.

We encourage young people not to share unnecessary personal information online and to seek support from a trusted adult when appropriate.

We do not ask girls to share sensitive personal information simply to belong to the community.

10

Data Retention

We aim not to keep personal information for longer than it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected.

The exact retention period may depend on how the information was collected, the purpose for which it was needed, and whether an external platform or service stores it.

11

Your Choices

You can choose what information you share with us. You can also contact us if you have questions about information you have previously shared.

Where appropriate and reasonably possible, you may ask us to correct, update, or remove information that you have provided.

Some information may be controlled by an external platform, in which case you may need to use that platform's own privacy controls.

12

Third-Party Services

Girls' Unplugged may rely on third-party platforms for communication, meetings, forms, social media, hosting, or other activities.

These services operate independently and may collect or process information according to their own policies.

13

Changes to This Policy

As Girls' Unplugged grows, the way we use technology and services may change.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices change or when additional services are introduced. The latest version will be published on this page.

14

Questions About Privacy?

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, something you've shared with us, or how a Girls' Unplugged activity handles information, please reach out.

We'd rather you ask than wonder.
