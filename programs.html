WHAT WE DO

We create spaces where girls can bloom & glow.

From weekly check-ins and game nights to honest conversations and community calls, our programs are designed around one thing: creating meaningful experiences for girls to connect, learn, laugh, and grow.

Join the Sisterhood Explore the Circle

MORE THAN ACTIVITIES

Every program has a purpose.

We are not interested in filling a calendar just for the sake of having things to do.

Every activity inside Girls’ Unplugged is created to help girls feel more connected, confident, supported, informed, or simply a little happier.

Some days are for serious conversations. Some are for learning. Some are for checking in with yourself. And some are simply for laughing with your girls.

Because growth does not always look serious. Sometimes, growth looks like finding people you can genuinely be yourself around.

WEEKLY VIBES

Something to look forward to every week.

Our weekly activities give Bloomies little moments throughout the week to pause, connect, reflect, laugh, and keep growing together.

MONDAY

Motivation Monday

We start the week with encouragement, reminders, affirmations, and little pushes to help every Bloomie step into the new week with intention.

Start strong

WEDNESDAY

Wellness Wednesday

A midweek pause to talk about emotional wellness, self-care, healthy habits, confidence, and the things we sometimes forget to check in on.

Check in

FRIDAY

Fun Friday

Because sisterhood should be fun too! We play games, laugh, vibe, connect, and create those random little memories that make a community feel like home.

Come vibe

SATURDAY

Reflection Saturday

A softer space to look back on the week, celebrate small wins, think about what we learned, and enter the next week with a little more clarity.

Slow down

CONVERSATIONS

The conversations girls actually want to have.

We create room for honest conversations about the things girls are experiencing, wondering about, learning, and sometimes afraid to say out loud.

VOICE

Honest Conversations

Real conversations about growing up, friendships, confidence, identity, emotions, relationships, dreams, and everyday life.

LEARN

Girl-Led Topics

Girls get a say in what we talk about. Topics and conversations can be shaped by what the community wants to learn or discuss.

GROW

Conversations That Stay With You

The goal is not just to have a conversation and move on. We want girls to leave with something useful: a new perspective, a reminder, a skill, or simply the feeling that they are not alone.

GUEST CONVERSATIONS

Bring in the voices. Ask the questions.

On the first Friday of each month, we create space for a guest conversation where girls can learn directly from people with different experiences, careers, stories, and perspectives.

These conversations take place virtually on Google Meet and give Bloomies the opportunity to listen, ask questions, share their thoughts, and connect.

When First Friday of every month
Time 7:00 – 8:30 PM WAT
Format Virtual conversation on Google Meet
Topics Shaped by what the community wants to hear

QUARTERLY COMMUNITY CALLS

Sometimes, we need to actually see each other.

Our quarterly community calls give Bloomies a chance to come together beyond the everyday group chat.

It is a bigger moment to reconnect, catch up, share experiences, celebrate progress, have fun, and remind ourselves that the sisterhood is made up of real girls in different places who are growing together.

Different quarter, different energy. But always the same goal: connection.

GAME NIGHTS

No deep conversation required.

Sometimes the best community moment is just everybody being unserious together. 😭

PLAY

Games & Challenges

Interactive games, challenges, prompts, quizzes, and activities that get everyone involved.

CONNECT

Meet Your Girls

Fun activities make it easier to start conversations, meet new people, and build friendships without the pressure.

LAUGH

Just Have Fun

There is no productivity goal here. Sometimes we simply need a space to laugh, play, and enjoy ourselves.

BEYOND THE WEEKLY ROUTINE

When there is something worth doing, we create it.

Girls’ Unplugged is still growing, which means our programs can grow with the needs and ideas of the community.

01

Special Community Activities

From themed conversations to community challenges, we create activities around moments that matter to the girls.

02

Awareness & Learning

We can use the community as a space to share useful information, opportunities, resources, and conversations around issues affecting girls.

03

Girl-Led Ideas

If a Bloomie has an idea that could make the community better, we want there to be room for that idea to become something real.

04

Community Collaborations

We are open to thoughtful collaborations with speakers, organisations, mentors, and people who genuinely care about creating better spaces for girls.

OUR APPROACH

We build with girls, not just for girls.

The best community experiences come from listening to the people who are actually part of the community.

01

Listen

We pay attention to what girls are saying, asking, experiencing, and needing.

02

Ask

We create room for girls to share ideas, suggest topics, and shape community experiences.

03

Create

We turn those ideas into conversations, activities, resources, and experiences.

04

Grow

We learn from every experience and keep improving the community together.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

The program is never the whole point.

A Monday post is just a Monday post. A game night is just a game night. But the connection built through those little moments can become something much bigger.

A girl feels less alone.

She finds other girls who understand parts of what she is going through.

A girl finds her voice.

She gets comfortable sharing her thoughts, asking questions, and participating.

A girl finds her people.

What started as a group chat becomes a community where she feels like she belongs.
