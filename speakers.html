THE VOICES WE BRING IN

Real people. Real stories. Real conversations.

We invite people with different experiences, perspectives, careers, and stories to have honest conversations with the girls in our community.

Meet the Speakers Become a Speaker

WHY SPEAKERS MATTER

Sometimes, hearing someone's story changes what you think is possible.

Girls are constantly imagining who they might become. Sometimes they need information. Sometimes they need advice. And sometimes they simply need to hear someone say, “I have been there too.”

Our guest conversations bring different voices into the Circle so girls can learn from people outside their immediate environment.

We care less about having impressive titles and more about having conversations that girls can actually take something from.

OUR CONVERSATIONS

It is not just about who you are.

It is also about what you can bring into the room.

EXPERIENCE

Something real to share

We welcome people who can share useful experiences, lessons, mistakes, perspectives, or stories that can help girls navigate their own journeys.

HONESTY

More than a perfect story

We do not expect speakers to present a perfect life. Honest lessons and real experiences can make conversations much more meaningful.

CONNECTION

A willingness to listen

Our conversations work best when speakers are open to questions, different viewpoints, and genuine interaction with girls.

WHAT WE TALK ABOUT

The topics can go anywhere girls need them to.

Our conversations can cover personal growth, careers, creativity, leadership, wellbeing, technology, relationships, and much more.

01

Confidence & Identity

Conversations that help girls think about confidence, self-expression, identity, boundaries, and becoming comfortable with who they are.

02

Education & Careers

Stories, lessons, and practical perspectives around education, career journeys, skills, opportunities, and navigating the future.

03

Leadership & Voice

Exploring how girls can use their voices, take initiative, lead with purpose, and contribute to the communities around them.

04

Wellness & Life

Honest conversations about emotional wellness, friendships, relationships, self-care, challenges, and everyday life.

05

Technology & Creativity

Making room for conversations around technology, creativity, digital skills, innovation, and the different ways girls can create.

06

Anything Girls Want to Ask

Sometimes the most important conversation is the one that starts with a question nobody thought to put on the agenda.

INSIDE A SESSION

Less lecture. More conversation.

Our guest sessions are designed to feel like conversations, not classrooms.

01

Arrive

We give everyone time to join, settle in, meet each other, and get comfortable.

02

Connect

We introduce the guest and create a relaxed starting point for the conversation.

03

Learn & Talk

The main conversation gives girls room to listen, learn, reflect, and participate.

04

Ask

We close with questions and space for girls to engage directly with the speaker.

THE DETAILS

One evening. One conversation. Lots to take away.

Our regular guest conversations are held on the first Friday of each month.

Day First Friday of the month
Time 7:00 – 8:30 PM WAT
Platform Google Meet
Community input Girls help shape the topics we discuss

The first 15 minutes are for arrival and introductions, while the final 15 minutes are reserved for questions and closing.
