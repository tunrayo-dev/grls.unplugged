THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE CIRCLE

Girls' Unplugged is built by girls who care.

Behind every conversation, game night, guest session and community moment are people who believe girls deserve spaces where they can be fully themselves.

Meet the Team Get Involved

MORE THAN A TITLE

There is a person behind the work.

Girls' Unplugged is a community first. That means the people helping to build it are not here just to hold fancy titles.

We are here to listen, create, organise, encourage, support and make sure the Circle continues to feel like a real space for girls.

Some people help behind the scenes. Some lead conversations. Some bring ideas. Some create the fun. And some simply show up and make the community better.

Everyone has a part to play.

HOW WE WORK

Titles matter less than how we show up.

The team behind Girls' Unplugged works around a few simple ideas that shape how we build the community.

01

We listen.

We pay attention to what girls are saying, asking for and experiencing so the community can grow around real needs.

02

We create together.

Girls are not just an audience here. Their ideas, questions and interests help shape what happens inside the Circle.

03

We keep learning.

We know we will not get everything perfect. We learn from the community, improve and keep building.

WHAT WE VALUE

The energy we bring into the room.

Whether we are planning a session or simply checking in with a Bloomie, these values guide how we show up.

01

Respect

Every girl deserves to be heard and treated with dignity.

02

Kindness

We choose compassion, especially when someone is having a difficult day.

03

Creativity

We make room for new ideas, different perspectives and unexpected ways to connect.

04

Accountability

We take our responsibilities seriously and learn when something can be done better.

05

Sisterhood

We want girls to know they do not have to navigate every season alone.

06

Growth

We are building too. We give ourselves permission to learn, change and become better.

A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

I didn't want to build a community that only looked good from the outside.

I wanted to create something that actually felt good to be part of.

A place where a girl could join a conversation after a long day, laugh over something completely unserious, learn something new, ask a question she had been scared to ask, or simply realise that another girl somewhere else understood her.

Girls' Unplugged is still growing, and I am learning along the way too. But the heart of it has remained the same: girls should have spaces where they can connect, grow and be themselves.

With love,
Motunrayo Linda Idowu
